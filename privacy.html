Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Cerebrum is designed to collect as little as possible. Here is what actually happens when you use it.

What we don't do

  • We do not use tracking pixels, third-party analytics, or advertising networks.
  • We do not require an account, email address, or personal information.
  • We do not sell, share, or profile user data. There is nothing to sell.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking. Cerebrum stores your preferences (theme, saved articles) in your browser's local storage; that data never leaves your device.

What is processed when you submit a search

  • Your question is sent to Cerebrum's server so it can query scholarly databases and generate an answer.
  • The question is forwarded to the scholarly APIs Cerebrum uses (Europe PMC, PubMed, OpenAlex, Crossref, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, and others). These APIs receive the search terms only.
  • The question is also sent to a language model provider (OpenRouter or Cloudflare Workers AI) to generate the summary. That provider processes the text and returns a response; per their policies, this data is not used to train their models.
  • Your IP address is visible to our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) as part of standard web traffic, and is used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • We do not permanently store your questions on our servers.

Local browser storage

Cerebrum uses your browser's localStorage to remember your saved articles, session history, and preferences (theme, motion settings, voice choice). This information is stored only on your device. Clearing your browser storage removes it entirely.

Optional integrations

Some optional features rely on third-party services and are disabled by default. If you choose to enable them:

  • ElevenLabs (text-to-speech): if you paste an ElevenLabs API key into Cerebrum's settings, it is stored locally in your browser and used to fetch narration audio. The key never leaves your device except when your browser sends it to ElevenLabs.

Children

Cerebrum is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page.
