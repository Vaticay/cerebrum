Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cerebrum collects as little data as physically possible. No tracking. No accounts. No data sales — there is nothing to sell.

What we don't do

  • No tracking pixels, third-party analytics, or ad networks.
  • No account, email, or personal information required.
  • No selling, sharing, or profiling of user data.
  • No tracking cookies. Preferences stay in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

What happens when you search

  • Your question is sent to Cerebrum's server to query databases and generate an answer.
  • Search terms are forwarded to scholarly APIs (Europe PMC, PubMed, OpenAlex, etc.). These APIs receive the search terms only.
  • The question is sent to a language model provider (OpenRouter or Cloudflare Workers AI) to write the summary.
  • Your IP address is visible to Cloudflare for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • We do not permanently store your questions.

Local storage

Saved articles, session history, and preferences (theme, motion, voice) are stored in your browser via localStorage. Clearing your browser data removes them entirely. This data never leaves your device.

Optional integrations

If you paste an ElevenLabs API key into Settings for premium text-to-speech, the key is stored locally and sent only to ElevenLabs when you request narration. It never touches Cerebrum's servers.

Children

Cerebrum is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
