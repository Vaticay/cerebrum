About Cerebrum

A research instrument that searches the real literature and gives you answers you can trace to the source.

What it does

You ask a scientific question. Cerebrum fans the query out to 16 open scholarly databases in parallel, scores what comes back for genuine relevance, and writes a summary where every claim is linked to the paper it came from. Every citation is a real DOI you can open and verify.

If no papers are found, Cerebrum says so. It will never invent a source.

The databases

  • Europe PMC — 43M+ biomedical articles
  • PubMed — 36M+ articles via NCBI E-utilities
  • OpenAlex — 250M+ cross-disciplinary works
  • Semantic Scholar — 220M+ papers with AI-generated summaries
  • Crossref — 150M+ DOI-registered works, retraction checking
  • arXiv, bioRxiv, medRxiv — preprints in physics, biology, medicine
  • DOAJ, PLOS, Zenodo, CORE, DataCite — open access coverage

What it is not

  • Not a replacement for reading the papers. Every answer is AI-generated. Verify anything you plan to cite or act on.
  • Not medical, legal, or financial advice. Cerebrum is a literature search tool. Consult a professional for decisions that affect your health, rights, or finances.
  • Not tracked or monetized. No ads. No account. No analytics. No data sales. There is nothing to sell.
